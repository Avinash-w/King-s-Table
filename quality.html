Care & Maintenance Tips

  • Washing: Use non-abrasive soft sponges or standard dishwashers. Avoid harsh metal scrubbers to preserve matte glaze integrity.
  • Microwaving: Safe for heating food and beverages. Handle with oven mitts when heating items for prolonged durations.
  • Stacking: Stack plates evenly by size. Place soft felt or paper dividers between high-gloss platters for storage.
